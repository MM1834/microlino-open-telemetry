# SES-001 — Cognito Domain Email Delivery

> **Status:** Cognito SES sender deployed; password-recovery acceptance pending
>
> **Date:** 2026-08-20

## Objective

Replace Cognito's low-volume shared `no-reply@verificationemail.com` delivery
with authenticated MOT-domain email suitable for controlled growth toward about
50 portal users and for recognizable support communication.

## Delivery identity

- SES identity: `microlino-open-telemetry.ch` in `eu-north-1`;
- visible sender: `MOT Portal <support@microlino-open-telemetry.ch>`;
- reply-to: `support@microlino-open-telemetry.ch`;
- authentication: SES Easy DKIM with 2048-bit keys;
- existing domain DMARC remains `p=reject` and must not be weakened.

The support mailbox must exist and remain protected independently of AWS. SES and
Cognito never receive its mailbox password.

## Staged rollout

1. Deploy the SES domain identity while Cognito remains `COGNITO_DEFAULT`.
2. Publish the three exact CloudFormation DKIM CNAME outputs at the DNS host.
3. Wait for SES identity and DKIM status `SUCCESS`.
4. Request and receive SES production access in `eu-north-1`; sandbox mode is not
   accepted for beta-user invitations.
5. Deploy `CognitoEmailSendingAccount=DEVELOPER` through a reviewed Change Set.
6. Resend the demo invitation and verify From, DKIM, SPF/DMARC result, reply path,
   first-password flow and password recovery.

The template deliberately omits Cognito `EmailConfiguration` in default mode and
therefore cannot switch prematurely merely because the SES resource exists.

## Cost and abuse boundary

Use shared SES sending, not dedicated IPs. Keep public self-registration disabled,
retain administrator-only invitations and monitor bounce/complaint reputation.
At the expected volume, message charges remain usage-based and no continuously
running service is introduced.

## Deployment evidence

The additive 2026-08-20 CloudFormation Change Set added only
`CognitoEmailIdentity`; no existing resource changed or required replacement and
Cognito remained `COGNITO_DEFAULT`. Hosttech published all three Easy-DKIM CNAME
records, SES reports `VerifiedForSendingStatus=true` and `DKIM=SUCCESS`, and a
direct transactional delivery from `MOT Portal <support@microlino-open-telemetry.ch>`
to the demo mailbox was received.

The SES production-access request is stored as `TRANSACTIONAL` with public
self-registration disabled, an expected initial cohort of about 50 invited users,
low monthly volume and explicit bounce/complaint handling. AWS review status is
`GRANTED`; the account is out of the sandbox with a 50,000-message daily quota and
14-message/second rate limit.

A second reviewed Change Set modified only
`DashboardUserPool.EmailConfiguration`, without replacement, and completed with
`CognitoEmailSendingAccount=DEVELOPER`. The effective sender is
`MOT Portal <support@microlino-open-telemetry.ch>`, reply-to is `support@…`, and
the SourceArn is the verified MOT-domain SES identity. The renewed demo invitation
was received and the first-password flow completed. Password-recovery delivery is
the remaining user acceptance.

Domain, DNS, operational checks and the deliberate boundary between Cognito/SES
mail and application/SNS notifications are maintained in the
[email delivery runbook](../../administrator/aws/email-delivery.md).
